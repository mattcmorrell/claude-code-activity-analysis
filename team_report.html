How does this work?

Prompt caching: Claude Code saves your conversation in a cache so it doesn’t have to re-read everything on every turn. The default cache expires after 5 minutes of inactivity, but this report assumes the 1-hour extended TTL is enabled ("CLAUDE_CODE_USE_EXTENDED_CACHE_TTL": "true"), so gaps under an hour don’t cost you anything.

Cache expiry: When the cache expires (you were idle more than an hour), Claude has to rebuild it from scratch. This is expensive — the 1-hour TTL write costs 20× more than reading from cache. This is the main source of “wasted” spend.

Why big sessions cost more: Every turn reads your full conversation. A session at 200K tokens reads all 200K per turn. At $0.50/MTok, that’s $0.10 just in reads — before Claude even responds.

The 1-hour rule: Breaks under an hour are free — the cache just sits there. For longer breaks, type /compact before stepping away to shrink the session, or /clear to start fresh — cheaper than Claude reloading a huge conversation.
